# CreatorOS: Data Deletion Instructions

_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

You can delete the data CreatorOS holds about you at any time.

## If you are a creator using CreatorOS
1. Sign in to CreatorOS.
2. Go to **Settings → Privacy & account**.
3. Choose **Delete account** and confirm with your password.

This permanently deletes your profile, conversations, deals, drafts, campaigns, invoices and any connected-account access tokens (including Instagram). A minimal record that an account was deleted, without personal data, is kept for security.

To remove only the Instagram connection: **Settings → Integrations → Instagram DMs → Disconnect**. You can also remove CreatorOS from Instagram: **Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites → Active → CreatorOS → Remove**.

## If you are not a CreatorOS user (for example a brand that messaged a creator)
Email **[your contact email]** with the subject "Data deletion request" and include your Instagram username or email address. We will delete the messages and details we hold about you within 30 days and confirm by email.
