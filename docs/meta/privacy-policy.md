# CreatorOS Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

CreatorOS ("we", "us") is a service that helps independent content creators in India find brand sponsorships, write and send pitches, and manage negotiations with brands. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

**Contact:** [your contact email]

## Who this applies to
- **Creators** who sign up for CreatorOS and connect their accounts (for example email or an Instagram professional account).
- **Brands and their representatives** who exchange messages with a creator through CreatorOS.

## What we collect

**From creators**
- Account details: name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash).
- Profile details you enter: niches, platforms, audience figures, rates and deal preferences.
- Connected Instagram professional account: account id, username, account type, and an access token issued by Instagram. The token is stored encrypted.
- Messages and drafts in conversations with brands, and the deal terms recorded from them.

**From Instagram direct messages (only when a creator connects Instagram)**
- We read new direct messages on the creator's Instagram professional account to find brand collaboration requests.
- Messages that are about a brand collaboration are stored so the creator can reply and manage the deal: the message text, time, and the sender's Instagram username and Instagram-scoped id.
- Messages from anyone else (fans, friends, personal chats) are **not stored**. We keep only an internal marker that the conversation was checked, without its content or the sender's name.
- Messages sent before the creator connected Instagram are not read.

**From brands**
- Business contact details published on a brand's own website, and messages a brand sends to a creator through CreatorOS.

## How we use it
- To show creators brands that may sponsor them, and to draft pitches and replies.
- To read brand replies and offers, and to prepare responses that the creator approves (or that their own automation settings allow).
- To keep a record of deals, campaigns and invoices for the creator.
- To keep the service secure and working.

We do **not** sell personal data, and we do not use Instagram data for advertising.

## AI processing
Drafts and the analysis of brand messages are produced with the help of AI models from third-party providers (currently Google Gemini; Anthropic Claude may be used later). Message content is sent to these providers only to produce the requested output. Every message sent on a creator's behalf says that it was AI-assisted.

## Sharing
We share data only with service providers that run CreatorOS for us (hosting, database, email delivery, AI providers), and with Meta/Instagram when we send a message on a creator's behalf. We disclose information if the law requires it.

## Retention and deletion
- Creators can delete their CreatorOS account at any time in **Settings → Privacy & account**. This deletes their profile, conversations, deals, drafts and connected-account tokens.
- Creators can disconnect Instagram at any time in **Settings → Integrations**; the stored token is removed immediately, and they can also remove CreatorOS under Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites.
- See our Data Deletion Instructions for how to request deletion.

## Security
Passwords are hashed, access tokens are encrypted, and access to data is limited to the creator who owns it.

## Your rights
Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Write to [your contact email].

## Changes
We will update this page when our practices change and change the date above.
